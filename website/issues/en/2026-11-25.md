## What this issue covers {#intro}

More than twenty issues in, we've mostly covered how to get more out of GrokBot. Newcomers keep asking earlier questions, though: Does my subscription include GrokBot? Why does the sign-in page show Grok or xAI branding? Will the trial charge me? What happens when my usage runs out?

The official docs answer all of these, but the answers are spread over several pages. The X Subscription recently brought Cursor, Grok, and X together in one plan while older plans carry on, which makes it easier to get confused. This issue puts the access and billing details in order as five tips. For team and enterprise rollout, see [Issue 20](/issues/2026-11-20/). For the usage page and resets, see [Issue 17](/issues/2026-11-17/).

::: callout The one thing to remember
Check that your plan includes GrokBot, then sign in with the right account. Try the trial on small tasks, and set a limit on extra usage before you rely on it.
:::

Prices, plan names, and rules in this issue come from the official docs and can change at any time. Check the official pages again before you decide.

## Five tips you can use today {#tips}

Each tip comes with an example prompt. Replace the text in square brackets with your own and send it. Please handle payments, upgrades, and cancellations yourself on the official pages.

### Tip 1: Check whether your plan includes GrokBot {#tip1}

The official FAQ is direct about it: GrokBot has no separate subscription. It comes with these plans:

::: props
- The X Subscription: Premium, Super, and Ultra include GrokBot, for individuals and team seats. Plus and Free don't
- Legacy Cursor plans: the paid individual plans, Pro, Pro+, and Ultra, and self-serve Teams include it
- Linked Grok subscriptions: a legacy Cursor account can link an individual SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus, SuperGrok Heavy, or X Premium+ account
- Grok Business: a seat on a self-serve Grok Business plan works too
- Enterprise: contact your Cursor account team
:::

New customers start on The X Subscription. The official pricing page lists monthly prices of $30 for Premium, $100 for Super, and $200 for Ultra, before tax. Super and Ultra include larger weekly usage than Premium. Older plans continue, and switching is up to you. One thing to watch: once you combine accounts under The X Subscription, it's permanent. It can't be undone, and support can't undo it for you.

::: prompt
These are my current subscriptions: [list them, for example Cursor Pro and SuperGrok].
Using the official docs, tell me:
1. Which subscription gives me access to GrokBot.
2. If I switch to The X Subscription, which old subscriptions get cancelled, and which I have to cancel myself, and where.
3. Which steps can't be undone.
Just explain. Don't buy or change anything for me.
:::

### Tip 2: Sign in with the right account, and link Grok when you need to {#tip2}

GrokBot uses the shared xAI sign-in path, and there's no separate GrokBot login, so Grok or xAI branding on the sign-in page is normal. On desktop, choose **Sign in** and finish in your browser. The official docs are clear that signing in only confirms who you are. It doesn't link or combine your X, Grok, or Cursor accounts, and it doesn't switch your plan.

Some common cases:

::: steps
- Your company uses single sign-on | Sign in as usual. Your Cursor SSO setup applies.
- A Grok Business seat | In the browser, choose **Sign in with Grok** and use the Grok account that holds the seat.
- SuperGrok or X Premium+ | On desktop, open the Get Started with Grok Bot page and choose **Link Grok Account**. On the phone, choose **Link Grok Account** on the access screen, sign in to Grok, then choose **Finished Linking? Refresh My Status**.
- You already have access | Open **Settings → Usage & billing** and look under **Manage plan** for **Get access with Grok**. It appears only when linking would raise your usage.
:::

Two more things to know up front. First, GrokBot needs cloud data storage, so if your account is on Privacy Mode (Legacy), the app asks you to switch to Privacy Mode first. On a team, an admin changes it. Second, a link is permanent, and you can't remove or move it yourself. If you linked the wrong Cursor account, contact support with the account that got the link and the account that should have it.

::: prompt
I'm about to sign in to GrokBot for the first time. My access comes from [plan name].
Using the official docs, list the sign-in steps I should follow, mark any step where I must pick a specific account
and any step that's permanent, and remind me which email to confirm before I start.
:::

### Tip 3: Use the trial credit on small tasks first {#tip3}

Some accounts can try GrokBot before they pay. When the access screen shows **Start trial**, choose it. The trial is a usage credit with a 7-day window, and it never turns into a paid plan.

The official docs point out that the credit is drawn down by how much work your Bot does, measured in steps and tokens rather than the number of messages you send. One big task can use all of it, and used credit isn't restored. A safer approach:

::: steps
- Keep the scope small | Hand over one small job at a time, with a clear finish line.
- Check usage first | Before a large or long task, look at what's left on the plan screen.
- Hold off on routines | Scheduled tasks keep drawing on the credit, so run things by hand during the trial.
:::

On a free account, you can end the trial early with **Cancel Trial** in **Settings → Usage & billing** (on the phone, **Settings → Usage**). It costs nothing, but any remaining credit is removed and you can't start the trial again. A free trial started through the App Store in the iOS app is a different thing: Apple runs it, and it becomes a paid Apple subscription unless you cancel first.

::: prompt
I'm on the trial and want to see what you can do.
Suggest three tasks that use little usage and take under half an hour. For each, tell me
which apps it needs, roughly how many steps it takes, and where it counts as done.
Don't start yet. I'll pick one.
:::

### Tip 4: Know where your usage comes from {#tip4}

Usage works differently depending on your plan:

::: props
- The X Subscription: GrokBot, the Grok apps, and Cursor share one weekly usage pool instead of separate quotas. Prepaid credit covers usage past the pool. Check it under **Usage** at grok.com/dashboard
- Legacy Cursor plans: GrokBot usage is metered on your Cursor account, not on Grok or X. Check it in GrokBot under **Settings → Usage & billing**
- Team seats: on self-serve team plans, each paid seat has its own weekly pool, and seats don't share pools
:::

If a legacy Cursor account has both a Cursor plan and a linked SuperGrok, the usage doesn't add up. GrokBot uses whichever grants more weekly usage. The official tiers line up like this: Pro, SuperGrok, and X Premium+ are the same tier, Pro+ matches SuperGrok Plus, and Ultra matches SuperGrok Heavy. Both subscriptions keep billing on their own, and buying one doesn't cancel the other. If you upgrade SuperGrok after linking, allow up to 24 hours for your weekly usage to move to the new tier. You don't need to link again.

This also explains a common question: on a legacy plan, your SuperGrok limit doesn't match your GrokBot usage because they're two separate meters for two products.

::: prompt
Please tell me:
1. Where my GrokBot usage is counted right now, and where I can see it.
2. How much I've used this week, and when it resets.
3. Which kinds of tasks have used the most lately.
Just report. Don't pause or change any routines.
:::

### Tip 5: Put a limit on extra usage {#tip5}

When your weekly usage runs out, you can keep going with extra usage, but that costs more. To avoid a surprise bill, set a limit ahead of time:

::: props
- The X Subscription: at grok.com/dashboard/billing, under **Additional usage credits**, leave Auto Top-up off or set a monthly limit on additional usage
- Legacy Cursor plans: in GrokBot, set **Settings → Usage & billing → On-demand monthly limit**, or on the web set **Monthly Limit** under **Spending** at cursor.com/dashboard
:::

Two things to remember. First, the limit doesn't stop a Bot in the middle of a run, so a task that's already running can go a little past it. Second, on a legacy Cursor plan with on-demand usage off, GrokBot stops when weekly usage runs out and resumes when it resets, and your important routines stop with it.

::: prompt
I've set a monthly limit of [amount] on extra usage.
Please look over all my routines. Based on how often each runs and roughly how much each run uses,
estimate which ones are most likely to get stuck when usage runs out, and suggest which could run less often.
Only suggest. Don't change any settings.
:::

## Limits you should know {#limits}

::: props
- Combining is permanent: combining accounts under The X Subscription can't be undone, even by support
- Links can't move: once SuperGrok or X Premium+ is linked to a Cursor account, you can't remove or move it yourself
- Plans that can't link: SuperGrok Lite and Grok Enterprise can't link. A Grok Business seat uses Sign in with Grok instead of linking from the plan screen
- The email can't change: the email on a Cursor account can't be changed. To use another email, make a new account
- Refunds follow the plan: GrokBot has no separate subscription, so it has no separate refund path. Spent prepaid credit and used on-demand usage aren't refundable, and Apple or Google handles store purchases
:::

## Case study: a fictional freelance designer goes from trial to paid {#case}

A fictional example (not anyone's real setup): a freelance designer wants a Bot to sum up client feedback once a week. She has never subscribed to any related plan, so she plans to try it before deciding.

::: props
- Starting point: a free account only
- Goal: a client feedback summary every Monday morning
- Concerns: no automatic charges, and no bill over budget at the end of the month
:::

### Highlight: try, then buy, then set a limit {#case-flow}

::: steps
- Try | She chooses Start trial and has the Bot sum up last week's feedback once, by hand, to see the result and the usage.
- Pick a plan | Happy with it, she subscribes to Premium on the official page, signs in with the same account, and doesn't buy again in the phone app.
- Set a limit | Right after subscribing, she sets a monthly limit on additional usage on the billing page, then makes the feedback summary a routine that runs every Monday.
- Check back | After the first week, she asks the Bot roughly how much each run uses and decides whether to change how often it runs.
:::

::: prompt
I want to make [task, for example a weekly client feedback summary] a routine that runs once a week.
Before that, do it once by hand. When you're done, tell me roughly how many steps it took,
and whether running it at this size every week would squeeze my usage. Set up the routine only after I confirm.
:::

## Community picks: habits for access and spending {#community}

Here are a few habits for getting access and keeping costs in check. The first three come from the official docs; the last three build on them and are offered as suggestions.

### Don't pay for the same plan twice {#c1}

The official docs advise that if your plan already includes GrokBot, you sign in on the phone with the same account instead of buying a second subscription through the App Store or Google Play. For more on the phone app, see [Issue 14](/issues/2026-11-14/).

### Check sign-in errors yourself, then bring details to support {#c2}

The official advice is to make sure you're on the current version, start sign-in again from the app, and confirm you picked the right account if you have more than one. If it still fails, contact support with the account email you meant to use, the exact error, your platform and version, a screenshot, and the steps you took before the error.

### Give an upgrade a day {#c3}

The official docs say that after you upgrade a linked SuperGrok, the weekly usage can take up to 24 hours to update. If your usage hasn't changed right after upgrading, don't link again.

### Set the limit before the routine {#c4}

Routines keep running while you're away. Set the limit on extra usage first, then turn on scheduled tasks, so the bill holds no surprises.

### One email, one account {#c5}

The account email can't change, and links can't move. Decide which email to use before you sign up, and you'll save yourself trouble later.

### Check usage once a week {#c6}

Pick a day each week to ask your Bot about usage, so you can see which Bot or routine uses the most.
