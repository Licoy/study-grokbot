## What this issue covers {#intro}

When past issues covered the cloud computer, memory, and approvals, they all mentioned in passing that passwords belong in Vault, not in chat. But we never looked closely at what Vault holds, how a Bot uses it, or how money actually leaves your account when a Bot buys something for you.

The official docs have a page called "Vault and secure sign-in". This issue follows a simple order, save, sign in, fill, hide, and pay, and turns it into five tips you can use right away: save logins and cards in Vault, understand the order a Bot uses to sign in, use Secure Forms well, keep API keys in the secure secret card, and set rules for payments.

::: callout The one thing to remember
Passwords, codes, and keys never go in chat. Logins and cards go in Vault, one-off values go in a Secure Form, and API keys go in the secure secret card. Saving a card means it can fill in at checkout automatically, so if you want to approve every payment, don't save it.
:::

## Five tips you can use today {#tips}

Each tip comes with an example prompt. Replace the parts in square brackets with your own details and send it.

### Tip 1: Save logins, cards, and contact info in Vault {#tip1}

Open **Settings → Vault** in the desktop app or on your phone, choose **New item**, and pick what to save:

::: props
- Login: Enter the website, username or email, and password, then choose **Save login**. The Bot uses it to sign in to that site
- Card: Choose **Scan with camera** or **Upload a photo** to fill it from the card, or type it in, then choose **Save card**
- Contact info: Enter the name, address, phone, and email, then choose **Save contact info**. It's used for shipping and billing
:::

Every item has **Hints**, where you can say when to use it, such as "Use for work purchases." A Bot follows the hint when it chooses between entries. If you already use another password manager, choose **New item** → **Import from CSV…** on desktop and pick its export. The file is read on your computer and never uploaded, and it must be under 5 MB. Items already in your vault are skipped, and rows with a missing password or a website that isn't https aren't imported. The export holds your passwords unencrypted, so delete it once you're done.

::: prompt Example prompt · copy and send to your Bot
I just saved two cards in Vault. Please check how you'll use them:
1. List the nicknames and Hints you can see for each card, without reading out full card numbers.
2. Tell me which kinds of purchases will go on which card.
3. If a card has no Hints, remind me to add some.
:::

### Tip 2: Know the order a Bot uses to sign in {#tip2}

When a Bot needs to sign in to a website, it looks for credentials in a fixed order:

| Order | Source | Does the Bot see the value? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A login saved in Vault | No, it's filled straight into the page |
| 2 | A connected password manager, such as 1Password | No, you approve in 1Password and it fills the login |
| 3 | A Secure Form in the chat asking you | No, it's filled straight into the page |

When a site emails a one-time code, the Bot can read it from a connected Gmail or Outlook account or from its own inbox. If you let a Bot choose the password when it signs up for a site, Vault generates the password and saves the login, and the Bot never sees it. When a form can't do the step, such as single sign-on, a passkey, a CAPTCHA it couldn't get past, an approval on another device, a QR code, or a bank card verification, the Bot hands you the computer. Choose **Take over** on the request, complete only that step, then choose **I'm done** and tell the Bot to continue. The signed-in session stays on the computer for future tasks and is shared across your Bots.

::: prompt Example prompt · copy and send to your Bot
Please sign in to [a website] and download last month's statement:
1. Use the login in Vault first. If there isn't one, ask me with a Secure Form. Don't ask me to send a password in chat.
2. If a step needs a QR code or a passkey, tell me which step and I'll take over.
3. When you're done, just tell me the file name and the amount. Don't change any account settings.
:::

### Tip 3: Use Secure Forms, and choose whether to save {#tip3}

When a page needs something only you should provide, the Bot shows a **Secure Form** in the chat, one form per step. The form names the site it fills, your answers go straight into the page, and the Bot never sees them.

::: steps
- Keep it for next time | Check **Securely save to Bot's vault** to save the login, card, or contact info in Vault.
- Use it once | Leave it unchecked, and the value is used only in that chat and expires on its own.
- Forms without the checkbox | These save straight to Vault, so don't submit one if you don't want it saved.
- Check the result | **Filled into the page** means it worked. **Could not fill into the page** usually means the page moved before the form could fill it. The values still weren't shown to the Bot, so submit a new form if it asks again.
:::

Also, when you give a Bot a name, address, phone number, or email in chat, it saves it as contact info once the task is done and tells you what it saved. Tell it if you don't want something kept.

::: prompt Example prompt · copy and send to your Bot
From now on, when you need a login or payment details from me, follow these rules:
1. Always use a Secure Form. Never ask me to type them in chat.
2. Before showing a form, tell me which site, which step, and why you need it.
3. Assume I'm using it only once unless I say "save it".
Repeat these rules back to me first.
:::

### Tip 4: Keep API keys in the secure secret card {#tip4}

For an API key or another secret that isn't a login or card, wait for the Bot to show a **secure secret card**. The field is masked. Choose **Save securely**, and the card says **Saved**. The value isn't written into the chat or shown to the Bot. The docs are clear: never paste a password, one-time code, or API key into ordinary chat, a Shell command, or a file on the computer, and don't type secrets into a demonstration when you teach a task.

Saved values are write-only, so not seeing one doesn't mean it's gone:

::: props
- Vault: Passwords stay hidden, and cards show only their last four digits
- Secure secret card: The value isn't shown after it's saved, and the footer says **Stored securely, never shown to your Bot**
- Team Bot Secrets: Only the name and description are listed, and the value isn't shown even in Shell. If the name is still there, so is the value
- Changing a value: Choose **Replace**, paste the new value, and choose **Replace value**. You can't read the old value back
:::

One more thing: recovering, resetting, or updating the cloud computer removes installed apps and packages, so a secret you typed into a file, a shell profile, or an installed tool can disappear with them. Secrets saved with the secure card, under Secrets, or in Vault are not affected.

::: prompt Example prompt · copy and send to your Bot
I need you to call the [service] API:
1. Show me the secure secret card so I can save the key. Don't ask me to send it in chat.
2. Never print the key or write it into a file.
3. Start with one read-only request and tell me whether it worked.
:::

### Tip 5: Set rules for paying on your behalf {#tip5}

A card saved in Vault fills in at checkout, and no setting requires your approval for each use. If you want to approve every payment yourself, don't save the card. When a Bot asks for a card with a Secure Form at checkout, leave the save box unchecked. If you already saved one, select it in **Settings → Vault** and choose **Remove**.

::: props
- Order cards: When a Bot buys something for you through an order card, it pays with a card saved in Vault after you tap **Pay**. If you haven't saved one, the Bot first asks for a card with a Secure Form. A card you don't save gets no order card, and the Bot asks you to approve the exact total in the chat instead
- Batch payments: One order card can cover up to 500 card payments. One approval covers payments for 24 hours, and the Bot stops if a card is declined, then tells you what was paid and what failed
- Wallets: **Payment accounts** lets you connect wallets such as Link, X Money, or Shop Pay, and **Default payment method** sets what Grok Bot pays with first. Wallets need one order card per payment
- Link one-time cards: With Link connected, a Bot can ask you to approve a one-time card for a single purchase. The request shows the merchant, items, and total. Choose the **Pay** button, which shows the amount, to approve, **Pay another way** to skip the card, or **Deny**. Nothing is charged if you decline or the request expires
:::

::: prompt Example prompt · copy and send to your Bot
From now on, whenever money is involved, follow these rules:
1. Before ordering, list the merchant, items, quantities, and total, and wait for me to reply "confirm".
2. For any total over [$20], don't use a saved card. Ask me for a card with a Secure Form instead.
3. After paying, tell me how much you paid and the last four digits of the card you used.
Repeat these rules back to me first.
:::

## Limits you should know {#limits}

::: steps
- Version | Vault needs Grok Bot 0.75.2 or later.
- Shared across your account | Every Bot on your account can use what you save in Vault, and you can't limit an item to one Bot.
- Team Bots can't use your Vault | A Team Bot's logins are added in its own settings by someone who owns or manages it, and its keys go under its Secrets, where everyone who can chat with it will use them.
- Some changes happen only in Settings | Passwords, usernames, and websites change only in Settings → Vault, and logins and cards are removed only there. Saved cards can't be edited on the phone yet.
- Saved cards fill in automatically | There's no per-use approval switch, so if you want to check every payment, don't save the card.
- Secrets stay out of chat | Don't paste passwords, codes, or keys into chat, Shell commands, or files, and don't type them during a demonstration.
:::

## Case study: how a fictional "Purchasing Assistant" helps a small studio {#case}

Continuing our fictional cast (not anyone's real setup): a five-person design studio has one partner who handles day-to-day purchasing. She set up a Bot called "Purchasing Assistant" to order supplies, renew software, and sort invoices.

| Step | What she does | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Setup | Saves the company card and shipping address in Vault, with the hint "Studio purchases" | Keeps personal cards out to avoid mix-ups |
| Logins | Imports supplier logins from her old password manager | Deletes the CSV afterwards |
| Ordering | Purchasing Assistant compares prices and sends an order card | Checks the merchant and total before Pay |
| Large orders | Anything over budget uses a Secure Form instead | Leaves the save box unchecked |
| Bookkeeping | Puts the week's orders and invoices in a sheet | Lists only the card's last four digits |

### Highlight: one order card restocks the week {#case-flow}

On Friday afternoon she taps Pay once, and next week's paper and ink are on their way.

::: flow
- Me | Say what supplies we need | person
- Purchasing Assistant | Compares prices and sends an order card
- Me | Check it and tap Pay | person
- Purchasing Assistant | Places the order and files the invoice
- Supplier | Ships the order | person
---
- Me → Purchasing Assistant: We're almost out of printer paper and black ink. Get a week's worth
- Purchasing Assistant → Me: I compared three stores. This one's cheapest, and the order card is ready
- Me → Purchasing Assistant: Approved
- Purchasing Assistant → Supplier: Order placed, shipping to the studio address
- Purchasing Assistant → Me: Ordered, and the invoice is in this week's sheet
:::

Later she had Purchasing Assistant list the studio's software subscriptions at the end of each month and flag the ones about to renew, so she can decide what to keep.

::: prompt Example prompt · copy and send to your Bot
You're this "Purchasing Assistant". When you buy things, follow these rules:
1. Use only the card and address whose Hints say "Studio purchases".
2. Send an order card before every purchase, with the merchant, items, and total.
3. When a step needs me to sign in or verify, say which site and which step.
4. In the bookkeeping sheet, record only a card's last four digits, and never record passwords or codes.
Repeat the rules back to me first.
:::

## Community picks: common ways to use it {#community}

Here are some common setups that are easy to start with. They're general ideas, so swap in your own details.

### Move in one go {#c1}

Export a CSV from your old password manager, import it into Vault on desktop, then ask your Bot to list the sites it can sign in to so you can spot old ones and remove them.

### Snap a card to save it {#c2}

When you attach a photo of a card in chat, Grok Bot offers **Save to Vault** so your Bot can pay with it later. Skip it for cards you don't want used automatically.

### Cancel a subscription {#c3}

Have your Bot sign in to a subscription site, find the cancel option, and send you the steps and a screenshot to confirm before it cancels.

### A secrets check-up {#c4}

Ask your Bot to look through recent chats and files on the computer for keys pasted by mistake. If it finds any, change them at the service and save the new ones with the secure secret card.

### A spending limit {#c5}

Give your Bot an amount limit: below it, use the saved card with an order card, and above it, always ask for a card through a Secure Form.
