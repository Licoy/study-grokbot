## Glossary {#glossary}

::: props
- Bot: An AI teammate with its own name, responsibilities, and memory. Works best when it owns one repeatable outcome.
- Memory: Preferences, role context, and work summaries saved separately for each Bot.
- Routines: Automated tasks that run on a schedule or are triggered by events (covered in detail in Issue 2).
- Cloud computer: One computer shared by all your Bots; files and sign-in state are shared.
- Template: A shareable copy of a Bot's configuration, without your computer, sign-ins, or chat history.
:::
