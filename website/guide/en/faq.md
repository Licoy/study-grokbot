## About this site {#about}

### Is this an official website? {#official}

No. Study GrokBot is an unofficial, open-source community project and is not affiliated with SpaceXAI / xAI or the official Grok Bot.

### How often is it updated? {#cadence}

The plan is one issue per day. Issue files live in `website/issues/en/` (English) and `website/issues/zh/` (Chinese) in the repository.

### Can I contribute? {#contribute}

Yes! Open an issue or pull request on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Licoy/study-grokbot), or share your workflow on X with #StudyGrokBot.
